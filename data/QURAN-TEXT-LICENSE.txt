The file `quran-uthmani.json` in this directory contains the Uthmani Qur'an
text of the Tanzil Project, retrieved via the alquran.cloud API
(edition: "quran-uthmani").

Build step (see ../scripts/build-quran-text.js):
  - the stray BOM / zero-width no-break space is removed
  - the unnumbered opening basmalah is moved out of ayah 1 for the 112 surahs
    that carry it (Al-Fatihah and At-Tawbah excepted)
  - no letters or diacritics of the Qur'anic text itself are altered
